Governance transition — effective 15 June 2026
Effective 15 June 2026 — the CGIAR Board (formerly the Integrated Partnership Board / IPB) replaces the prior structure. The CGIAR Council replaces the System Council. The CGIAR Office replaces the System Management Office. The General Assembly of Centers is dissolved. The Board now comprises 19 members: 12 Center Board Chairs (ex officio, voting), 6 Independent Members incl. the Board Chair and AFRC Chair (voting), and the Executive Managing Director (non-voting). The Nominations Committee has moved to the Board.

Diligent Platform

A quick guide to the Diligent board portal — what it is, how to use it, and where to get help.

What is Diligent?

Diligent is the platform we use for issuing Board documents — board books, agendas, minutes, and comments on the books all live in one place.

What the platform does for you
  • Board books & materials — agendas, papers and minutes in one place, available online and offline.
  • Comments on board books — annotate and discuss materials directly within a document.
  • Mobile & offline access — review materials on phone or tablet, even without an internet connection.
How to use it
  1. Go to director.diligentboards.com — this is the Board's direct sign-in page, not Diligent's general product site.
  2. Sign in with the credentials provided by the Board Secretariat when your account was set up. If you don't have an account yet, contact the Secretariat.
  3. Your current board book and meeting materials appear on the home screen — open a meeting to see its agenda and papers.
  4. Use in-book search to jump straight to a specific paper or section instead of scrolling.
  5. Use comments to annotate or flag specific pages within a board book.
  6. Before travelling, download materials for offline reading via the Diligent Boards mobile app.
Installing the mobile app

On your phone or tablet, open the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) and search for "Diligent Boards". Install the app, then sign in with the same credentials provided by the Secretariat. Once signed in, your board books sync automatically and can be downloaded for offline reading before you travel.

Getting help

Start with the Secretariat — for account setup, password resets, access issues, or any question about using Diligent:

  • Board Secretariat: cgiar-board-secretariat@cgiar.org
  • Council Secretariat: cgiar-council-secretariat@cgiar.org

The Secretariat will resolve most issues directly, and will escalate to Diligent's support team on your behalf if needed — please don't contact Diligent support directly.

Self-service: Diligent system status page — check for any service outages before reporting an issue. New to the platform? The Diligent Training Hub has self-paced tutorials.

For any Diligent account setup, access, or usage issue, contact the Board Secretariat first — they will loop in Diligent support if needed.
